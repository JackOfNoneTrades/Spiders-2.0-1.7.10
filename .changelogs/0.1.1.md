* Added logo
* Renamed release jars to use the project name, Spiders-2.0-1.7.10.
* Added Modrinth and CurseForge publishing configuration.
* Added climbing and fall-damage compatibility for LOTR Legacy's Mirkwood, Mordor and Utumno ice spiders, preserving their faction AI, venom, size variants and player riding controls.
* Restricted LOTR spiders' attack leaps to the ground so they do not jump away from walls or ceilings.
* Fixed large spiders drifting away from surfaces, and aligned vanilla and LOTR jockeys with the spider on walls and ceilings, including suffocation, sight and arrow origins.
* Fixed LOTR spiders spawning inside walls and large spiders stalling at the base of narrow pillars.
* Fixed ceiling drops turning jockeys into blocks; spiders now turn upright gradually as the rider clears the surface.
* Added configurable movement speeds by spider entity ID or class name, with vanilla and LOTR defaults. Subclasses inherit entries, size and potion modifiers are preserved, and changes made in the config screen apply immediately to existing spiders.
* Fixed spiders endlessly turning on pillars when a nearby platform edge was mistaken for a connected climbing surface.
